Governance

Controls your finance and brand teams will ask about

Three levels — fully automatic, review the first N posts, or approve every post. Policies are set per channel, so Instagram can be fully automatic while LinkedIn requires sign-off. Any change is written to the audit log with the author's name and timestamp.

Upload a banned-claims list, competitor names to avoid, and mandatory disclaimers per regulated category. Any generated output matching the list is blocked before scheduling and flagged to the workspace owner with the triggering phrase highlighted.

Four built-in roles — owner, manager, contributor and viewer — plus per-workspace scoping for agency setups. Contributors can draft but not publish; viewers can see reports without accessing the asset library.

Workspace data is stored in the region you select at onboarding, with India as the default for domestic accounts. Full exports are available on demand in CSV and JSON, and the asset library exports in open image formats so you are never locked in.

Growth and Scale plans carry a 99.5% publishing-path availability commitment, measured monthly on successful scheduled publishes. Scale adds a named account manager and an out-of-hours on-call channel for campaign launches.
