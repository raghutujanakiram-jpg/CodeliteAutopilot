The AutoPilot workflow

Four stages from blank page to published pipeline

AutoPilot is built as an assembly line, not a chat window. Each stage produces a durable asset that the next stage consumes, so nothing is re-created by hand twice.

1

Design — brand asset generation

Enter your business name, choose a deliverable from the dropdown and drop a brief. AutoPilot produces logos, website landing pages, business cards, note cards, digital business cards, letterheads, email signatures, invoices and short animations in your brand palette.

2

Create — social media expansion

Establish your presence across Facebook Pages, Instagram Business, X, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Threads, TikTok Business, Google Business, YouTube Shorts, Discord, Telegram and Slack from one creation surface.

3

Connect — channel integration

The Connect Profiles dashboard authenticates and links each account. AutoPilot then maps all twelve profiles to the central engine so the pipeline can read performance and write content back.

4

Auto-post — the execution engine

Activate triggers and AutoPilot generates posts, reels, captions and an industry-specific calendar, publishes on schedule, produces SEO and ad copy variants, and localises output per market.
